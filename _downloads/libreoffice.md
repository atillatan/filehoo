LibreOffice is the free, open-source office suite used by millions. Writer, Calc, Impress, Draw, Base and Math cover everything from letters to complex spreadsheets, and read/write Microsoft Office formats reliably.

Key features:

* Writer (documents), Calc (spreadsheets), Impress (presentations)
* Opens and saves DOCX, XLSX, PPTX plus ODF standards
* Export to PDF with forms and signatures
* Hundreds of templates and extensions
* No subscription, no account, no telemetry to sell
* Open source, actively developed by The Document Foundation
