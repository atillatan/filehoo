Audacity is the free, open-source, multi-track audio editor and recorder. Record live audio, digitize tapes, trim podcasts, clean up noise and export to any common format.

Key features:

* Multi-track recording and non-destructive editing
* Noise reduction, compression, EQ and dozens of effects
* VST3, LV2 and Nyquist plugin support
* Import/export WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG and more
* Spectrogram view and analysis tools
* Free and open source across Windows, macOS, Linux
