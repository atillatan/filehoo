Steam is Valve's game platform and store, home to tens of thousands of PC games, from AAA titles to indie gems. The client manages your library, keeps games updated, syncs saves to the cloud and connects you with friends.

Key features:

* Massive store with frequent deep-discount sales
* Automatic game updates and cloud saves
* Friends, chat, voice, streaming and Workshop mods
* Remote Play: stream your games to other devices
* Family sharing and parental controls
* Big Picture mode and Steam Input for controllers
