ShareX is the most capable free screen capture tool on Windows. Capture any region, window or scrolling page, record video or GIF, annotate, then let automated workflows upload the result and copy the link to your clipboard.

* Region, window, scrolling and OCR capture
* Screen recording to MP4 or GIF
* Powerful annotation editor

* 80+ upload destinations
* Color picker, ruler, QR code and other tools; open source
