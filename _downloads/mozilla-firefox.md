Mozilla Firefox is the leading independent, open-source web browser. It blocks trackers by default, keeps your browsing data out of advertisers' hands, and remains one of the most customizable browsers available.

Key features:

* Enhanced Tracking Protection blocks trackers and cryptominers by default
* Total Cookie Protection isolates cookies per site
* Sync tabs, bookmarks and passwords across devices
* Thousands of add-ons and themes
* Picture-in-picture video, built-in screenshot tool, PDF editor
* Developed by the non-profit Mozilla Foundation
