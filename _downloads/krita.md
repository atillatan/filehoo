Krita is built for drawing and painting rather than photo editing: brush engines, stabilizers and a distraction-free canvas that rival paid illustration software, from the KDE community, completely free.

Key features:

* Hundreds of customizable brushes with a powerful brush engine
* Line-smoothing stabilizers for clean inking
* Full layer, mask and blending-mode support
* Animation timeline for 2D frame-by-frame work
* Opens and exports PSD, PNG, TIFF, EXR
* Free and open source, no subscription ever
