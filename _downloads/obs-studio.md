OBS Studio is what streamers and screencasters actually use. It captures your screen, camera and audio, mixes them in real time with scenes and transitions, and streams to Twitch or YouTube, or records locally in high quality. All completely free.

Key features:

* Unlimited scenes with sources: display, window, camera, browser
* Real-time audio mixer with filters (noise gate, compressor)
* Stream to Twitch, YouTube or any RTMP service
* High-quality local recording (mkv, mp4)
* Studio mode with transitions
* Plugin ecosystem and scripting
