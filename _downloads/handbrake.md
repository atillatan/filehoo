HandBrake converts video from nearly any format to modern, widely supported codecs. Shrink huge recordings, convert old files, or batch-process a whole season with presets tuned for every device.

Key features:

* Convert nearly any source to MP4, MKV or WebM
* H.264, H.265, AV1, VP9 encoders with GPU acceleration
* Built-in device presets (phones, tablets, TV, web)
* Batch queue, chapter markers, subtitles and audio tracks
* Filters: deinterlace, denoise, crop, scale
* Free, open source, cross-platform
