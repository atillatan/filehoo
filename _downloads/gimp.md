GIMP (GNU Image Manipulation Program) is the most complete free alternative to paid photo editors. Version 3 brings non-destructive filters, a modernized interface and better PSD compatibility.

Key features:

* Layers, masks, channels and paths
* Non-destructive filter editing (GIMP 3)
* Professional retouching and color tools
* Opens and exports PSD, PNG, JPEG, TIFF, WebP and dozens more
* Scriptable with Python; huge plugin ecosystem
* Free and open source forever
