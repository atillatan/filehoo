Thunderbird is a free, full-featured email client from the makers of Firefox. It handles multiple accounts, calendars, tasks, contacts, newsgroups and RSS feeds, with strong junk filtering and OpenPGP encryption built in.

Key features:

* Unified inbox for all your mail accounts (IMAP/POP3/Exchange via add-on)
* Built-in calendar, tasks and contacts
* End-to-end encryption with OpenPGP
* Adaptive junk mail filtering
* Tabbed email, quick filter bar and powerful search
* Extensible with themes and add-ons
