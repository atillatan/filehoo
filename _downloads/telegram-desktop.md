Telegram Desktop is the official Windows client for Telegram, one of the fastest and most feature-rich messengers. Your chats sync seamlessly across every device, and media is stored in Telegram's cloud so it never eats your disk.

Key features:

* Seamless sync across phone, tablet and desktop
* Groups up to 200,000 members, channels, polls and bots
* Send files up to 2 GB each, unlimited cloud storage
* Secret chats with end-to-end encryption (on mobile)
* Stickers, themes and powerful media editing
* Open-source client, no ads in chats
