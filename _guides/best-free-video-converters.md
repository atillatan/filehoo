## The three jobs it does perfectly

1. **Shrink huge recordings.** A phone or GoPro clip re-encoded with the H.265
   preset routinely drops to a third of its size with no visible loss.
2. **Make old files play anywhere.** Ancient AVI or WMV becomes a clean MP4
   that every TV, phone and browser accepts.
3. **Batch work.** Point it at a season of recordings, queue them all, walk
   away.

## Good companions

- **VLC** quietly includes a basic converter (Media, Convert/Save): fine for a
  single quick format change if you already have it installed.
  [Get VLC here](/downloads/vlc-media-player/).
- **FFmpeg** is the command-line engine underneath half the video tools on
  earth. Unmatched power, no interface; worth learning if you convert daily.

## What to avoid

- **Upload-based converter websites**: slow for big files, privacy-hostile,
  and ad-ridden.
- **"Free" desktop converters with watermarks** or 5-minute limits: the paid
  unlock funds the ads that got you there. HandBrake makes the whole category
  pointless.

## FAQ

**H.264, H.265 or AV1 - which should I pick?**
H.264 for maximum compatibility, H.265 for the best size/quality balance
today, AV1 if the videos are for the web and encoding time is not precious.

**Does HandBrake rip copy-protected DVDs or Blu-rays?**
No, it deliberately does not break copy protection. Unprotected home-made
discs convert fine.
